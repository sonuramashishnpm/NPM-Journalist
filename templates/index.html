1️⃣ Core Purpose

NPM_Journalist exists to:

  • Enable citizens to safely send complaints or messages to government officers without fear of being traced or targeted.
  • Provide ethical, responsible mediation so the message is safe, non-offensive, and constructive.
  • Reduce the power asymmetry between ordinary users and authority figures.

The central promise: You can speak truth or file a complaint without fear, and your message will only go through if it’s safe and respectful.

2️⃣ What it is NOT

  • It’s not a paid service.
  • It’s not a spam filter based on identity.
  • It’s not a platform for monetizing complaints.
  • It’s not a tool to bypass law or policy — it ensures civility and legality.

3️⃣ How it achieves this (technical vision)

  1. AI Validation
    • Validates complaint content.
    • Checks for offensive language or threats.
    • Responds Yes / No for safe delivery.
  2. Anonymity by design
    • No login, no payment, no identifiable tracking.
    • Only the complaint reaches the officer.
  3. Anti-spam without identity
    • One-shot submission.
    • Semantic similarity detection.
    • Officer-side quotas and reflective delays.
    • Cognitive friction for careful writing.
  4. Civic-first architecture
    • The platform never compromises user safety for revenue or convenience.
    • Focus on trust, integrity, and transparency.

4️⃣ Frontend / User Experience Vision

  • Minimal, clear UI with three sections: Send_Complain, About Us, Docs.
  • Default to Send_Complain.
  • Dropdown to select officer/post.
  • Rich-text editor for message composition (bold, italic, highlights).
  • Send button triggers AI validation → delivery.
  • Safe, intuitive, visually minimal with one main color + two secondary colors.

5️⃣ Revenue / Sustainability (optional, orthogonal)

  • Revenue must never touch user identity or complaint flow.
  • Can come from:
    • Local sponsorships (“Platform supported by …”)
    • NGO or civic organization backing
    • Ads that are contextual, not invasive
  • Core mission never compromised.

6️⃣ Core Vision Statement (one line)

“NPM_Journalist empowers citizens to safely, anonymously, and responsibly communicate with authorities, ensuring their voices are heard without fear of personal risk.”
